Conductor 1.1.2 finishes what 1.1.1 started: albums completed with the songs you don't have (⬇) from a bigger
edition than yours. Checked against Deezer itself this time: an edition with two takes of one of your songs ("Sticky
Fingers (Super Deluxe)" has "Wild Horses (2009 Mix)" and "(Acoustic Version)") counted it twice and looked like the
better match. Each of your songs now counts once, and both of Deezer's searches are always looked at. "Sticky Fingers"
shows its 10 songs. Albums are matched again the next time they're opened.

## Files
- Linux: `conductor-1.1.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` and `-aarch64`, and the packages: `conductor_1.1.2_amd64.deb` (and
  arm64), `conductor-1.1.2-1.x86_64.rpm` (and aarch64), `conductor-1.1.2-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst` (and aarch64).
- Windows: `conductor-1.1.2-windows-x86_64.zip`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and `docker pull ghcr.io/d-safro/conductor:1.1.2` (also `1.1` and `latest`).

Upgrading from 1.1 or 1.0: replace the program, the package or the image.
