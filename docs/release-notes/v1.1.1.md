Conductor 1.1.1 fixes one thing: albums completed with the songs you don't have (⬇) from a bigger edition than
yours. When Deezer's exact search finds only a deluxe edition ("Sticky Fingers (Super Deluxe)", 33 songs) and the
album itself ("Sticky Fingers (Remastered)", 10) comes up only in its plain search, Conductor now looks there too.
Albums matched since 1.1.0 are matched again the next time they're opened.

## Files
- Linux: `conductor-1.1.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` and `-aarch64`, and the packages: `conductor_1.1.1_amd64.deb` (and
  arm64), `conductor-1.1.1-1.x86_64.rpm` (and aarch64), `conductor-1.1.1-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst` (and aarch64).
- Windows: `conductor-1.1.1-windows-x86_64.zip`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and `docker pull ghcr.io/d-safro/conductor:1.1.1` (also `1.1` and `latest`).

Upgrading from 1.1.0 or 1.0: replace the program, the package or the image.
