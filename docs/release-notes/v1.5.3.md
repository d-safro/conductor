Conductor 1.5.3: albums are completed the new way right after upgrading.

## Fixes
- An album that an older Conductor found nothing for on Deezer was kept as "nothing" for a week, so 1.5.2's search
  under "Various Artists" (and 1.5.1's under the first-named artist) didn't reach it until then. Those are looked
  for again now.

- Windows: `conductor-1.5.3-windows-x86_64.zip`.
