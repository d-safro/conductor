Conductor 1.6.0: an album's song list is kept, and songs fetched for an album join it.

## New
- Opening an album that Conductor matched to Deezer reads the song list it kept the first time, not Deezer again
  (also after a restart, and once you have every song). Each song points to your copy, or to its download until it
  arrives.

## Fixes
- A song you fetch from an album of yours (⬇ on it) is tagged with that album's name, artist, year and genre and its
  disc and track number on it when it's downloaded, so it joins the album instead of starting one of its own with
  the tags of whoever shared it.
- Songs with no disc number are listed with disc 1, not before it.

## Files
- Linux: `conductor-1.6.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` and `-aarch64`, and the packages: `conductor_1.6.0_amd64.deb` (and
  arm64), `conductor-1.6.0-1.x86_64.rpm` (and aarch64), `conductor-1.6.0-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst` (and aarch64).
- Windows: `conductor-1.6.0-windows-x86_64.zip`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and `docker pull ghcr.io/d-safro/conductor:1.6.0` (also `1.6` and `latest`).

Upgrading from any 1.x: replace the program, the package or the image. The database gains two tables on first start.
