Conductor 1.1: sound levelling for every song, music folders and album downloads for apps, and the fixes for every
known bug left after 1.0. Nothing changes in how you run it: replace the program, the package or the image.

## New
- **Sound levelling for every song.** Apps that do ReplayGain (Encore, Symfonium, Feishin, …) already play tagged
  songs at the same loudness; now the songs whose files say nothing (most downloads) are measured too, in the
  background at the lowest priority, one at a time, and the apps get the measured gain. Tags in the files always win.
  Podium shows how far it is. Recital and share links level songs as well, never past a song's peak.
  Settings: `loudness_analysis` (on), `loudness_threads` (1).
- **Picking a music folder in your app** (Symfonium, Tempus, Ultrasonic, DSub, Feishin…) shows only that folder's
  music: lists, searches, random songs, genres and favourites.
- **Downloading a whole album or artist** from an app sends a zip of the original files, made as it's sent.

## Fixes
- **Albums are completed from their own edition** (a 10-song album was filled out from a 33-song deluxe one); albums
  matched wrongly before are matched again after the upgrade.
- **One conversion per song**, also when two devices ask for it at once; `transcode_cache_mb = 0` works.
- **Podcast episodes are converted** when the app asks for another format or a lower bitrate.
- **Raw `.aac` files** are `audio/aac`, not `audio/mp4`.
- **An admin can list another user's playlists** (`getPlaylists?username=`).
- **Stopping cleanly:** systemd and `docker stop` (SIGTERM) now let requests finish and close the database instead of
  killing Conductor; the Windows service tells Windows when it failed, so Windows starts it again.
- **Windows upgrades** with `install.ps1 -OpenFirewall` no longer add a firewall rule each time.
- **Podium's Settings** are saved whole (written aside, then swapped in) and one at a time, so two admins can't lose
  each other's change.
- **Docker's health check** asks the port Conductor really listens on (new `conductor health`).
- **The one-time setup link** is no longer written to the logs; `conductor setup link` shows it.
- **Removing the .deb or .rpm** reloads systemd.

## Files
- Linux: `conductor-1.1.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` and `-aarch64`, and the packages: `conductor_1.1.0_amd64.deb` (and
  arm64), `conductor-1.1.0-1.x86_64.rpm` (and aarch64), `conductor-1.1.0-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst` (and aarch64).
- Windows: `conductor-1.1.0-windows-x86_64.zip` (`install.ps1` installs or upgrades the service).
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and `docker pull ghcr.io/d-safro/conductor:1.1.0` (also `1.1` and `latest`).

Upgrading from 1.0: replace the program, the package or the image; the database is brought up to date when it starts.
