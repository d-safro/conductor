Conductor 1.2: live updates for apps, levelling for apps that can't level songs themselves, and conversions that
start sooner, tested on a (simulated) Raspberry Pi.

## New
- **Live updates** (`encore.events`): an app keeps one event stream open and hears at once when your other device
  sends a command or starts a song, a friend acts in your Jam, your settings or queue sync from another device, a
  playlist changes, or a scan runs. Each account hears only its own. Encore 1.15 uses it instead of checking every
  few seconds; other apps carry on as before.
- **Levelling in conversions** (`level_conversions`: off, track or album; off by default): for apps that don't apply
  ReplayGain themselves (a car stereo, a simple Subsonic app), a converted song is played at the same loudness as
  the rest, never past its peak. Apps known to level songs themselves (Encore, Symfonium, Feishin, Recital) aren't
  levelled twice; `level_conversions_clients` picks the apps by name instead. Songs sent as they are never change.

## Fixes
- **Converted songs start sooner**: ffmpeg held back about 15 seconds of the song before sending anything; the first
  bytes now come at once (on a Raspberry Pi 4 core, 0.9 s instead of 2 s).
- **Skipping through songs** no longer leaves each skipped song converting to its end: a conversion nobody listens to
  for 3 seconds stops, so the song you stay on starts in half a second instead of four.

## Small machines
Tested on a simulated Raspberry Pi 4 (and 5) with 3,000 songs: up to 8 apps converting at once start in about 2 s,
a light call answers in 40 ms, and Conductor itself uses 30-45 MB. Recommended settings are in
[docs/GUIDE.md](../GUIDE.md) ("Small machines").
