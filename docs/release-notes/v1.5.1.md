Conductor 1.5.1: albums by artists credited together are completed again.

## Fixes
- **Albums by artists credited together** ("Андрей Макаревич & Папоротник", "A feat. B", "A и группа B"): Deezer
  usually lists these under the first-named artist only, so the album's songs you don't have weren't shown. Now,
  when the whole name finds nothing, the first-named artist is tried too, and the rest of the album shows (and can be
  fetched) as on any other album.
