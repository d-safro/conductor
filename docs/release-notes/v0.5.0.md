Song radio, similar songs and similar artists that stay close without AI: Conductor now works out which artists go
together from your own library and what you play.

## New
- **Sounds like, without AI.** Before, without AudioMuse-AI, "similar songs" (what song radio is made of) leaned on
  genre tags and on related artists from Deezer, so in a library with sparse tags a radio could find two songs and
  wander off. Now every artist is weighed against the one you started from:
  - the artists you play in the same sittings (everyone's plays on the server);
  - related artists from online info, when it's on;
  - the same genres, and genres sharing a word ("Russian Rock" and "Rock");
  - the script their names and titles are written in, so a library in several languages doesn't jump between them;
  - their era.

  The closest artists come first, in turns with the artist's own songs, and the songs you've hearted or played come
  first within each artist. Similar artists (`getArtistInfo`) are filled the same way. It needs nothing extra
  (no AI, no online service) and answers in about 10-20 ms. AudioMuse-AI, when you have it, still comes first.

  On a real library with downloads and online info off, a radio from a Russian rock song now stays with Russian rock
  (АлисА, Чайф, Секрет…), where before it found two songs; one from Pink Floyd goes to the Beatles, the Stones,
  Dylan and Simon & Garfunkel.

## Fixes
- Related artists from Deezer were only looked up when downloads were on; online info is enough now.
- Genres written with "|" between them ("Rock | Blues | Hard Rock") are split like those with ";" or ",".

## Files
- `conductor-0.5.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`, `conductor-0.5.0-linux-aarch64.tar.gz`: one static program each. Unpack,
  then `./conductor setup`. Needs `ffmpeg` to convert formats.
- `conductor-0.5.0-windows-x86_64.zip`: `conductor.exe` with `install.ps1` to install it as a Windows service.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and the image: `docker pull ghcr.io/d-safro/conductor:0.5.0`.

Upgrading from 0.4.0: replace the program or the image; there's nothing to run. Your data stays as it is.
