Conductor 1.5: "sounds like" from the sound of your songs, with no AI to set up.

## New
- **"Sounds like" from the audio itself** (`audio_analysis`, on): Conductor listens to a minute of every song in the
  background, at the lowest priority after levelling (about a tenth of a second a song on a desktop, two on a
  Raspberry Pi, once), and works out its tempo, how percussive it is, its timbre, brightness and key. Song radio,
  Instant Mix, the mixes and "similar songs" now find songs that sound alike, blended with the artists you play
  together; `findSonicPath` walks from one song's sound to another's. Without AudioMuse-AI, apps get these answers
  from Conductor itself; with AudioMuse-AI set up, it's still asked first. Podium shows how far the analysis is.

## Changed
- **Every playlist change is heard live** (`encore.events`): also imports, ListenBrainz and Spotify playlists, the
  On trial playlist, and songs that arrive and join a playlist.

## Fixes
- Searching Soulseek for a request on a read-only server is refused at once (nothing could be picked).
